Privacy Policy

DMG Profile Widgets - Last updated: January 2026

What We Collect

When you connect your Notion account to DMG Profile Widgets, we access:

  • Your Notion user ID and email address
  • Your name and profile picture (if available)
  • Access to workspace pages you've granted permission to

How We Use Your Data

We use this information to:

  • Identify you within the DMG team widgets
  • Display your profile information on your personal dashboard
  • Show your schedule, meetings, and team information
  • Allow managers to view team rota submissions

Data Storage

Your authentication tokens are stored securely and used only to communicate with Notion on your behalf. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Your Rights

You can disconnect your Notion account at any time by:

  • Going to your Notion Settings → My connections
  • Removing the "DMG Profile Widgets" integration
