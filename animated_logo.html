The Master Sync Reset

What does this do?

This button performs a Hard Reset of your cloud database. It completely wipes all staff records and re-uploads the verified 40-member DMG hierarchy from scratch.

When should you press this?

  • If you see duplicate names in your directory or org chart.
  • If the management hierarchy becomes broken or incorrect.
  • If you want to quickly return to the canonical "Starting Point" structure.

How often should you use it?

Rarely. This is your "Break Glass" button. For daily changes, use the Personnel Intake tab or the Delete buttons in the directory.
